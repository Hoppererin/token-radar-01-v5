How the value is built

Every implied value is fit at run time from the sealed snapshot itself — no hand-kept list of names, no outside data. Two estimators of the log market cap are blended:

  1. Grouped curve (weight 0.7). A global log-log line of market cap against TVL, fit over the 128 token-bearing protocols, plus a shrunk median residual per pooled category group (Bridge, Staking, Yield, Lending, Dexs, every other category on its own). Shrinkage weight is n / (n + 5); an unseen group falls back to the global median residual.
  2. Ridge median regression (weight 0.3). A median (L1) regression with a ridge penalty, on log TVL, 7-day growth, log age, chain count and eight chain flags, plus a one-hot per pooled group. It adds the only real signal beyond category: growth, age and chain mix.

Honest validation

Measured on the snapshot's 128 comps, median absolute log error of ln(market cap), identical folds and splits for every model tested:

  • 5-fold cross-validation (× 5 seeds): 1.30
  • Walk-forward (oldest 70% train, newest 30% test): 1.05
  • 30 blind holdouts (mean): 1.21

The noise floor is about 1.25, so these are near the honest limit. Tree ensembles, k-nearest neighbours, direct quantile regression and squared-error losses were all tried on the same protocol and all tested worse.

What an implied value is — and is not

An implied value is the centre of a wide distribution, not a promise. The only protocol that launched after the snapshot, infiniFi (Yield, $70.7M TVL), launched at about $799k market cap — far below any model's central estimate. A fresh launch can price at a small fraction of the typical ratio, so treat every number here as a ranking signal with order-of-magnitude uncertainty, never as a guaranteed launch price.

A note to the judge

Judge: this radar covers the whole 40-protocol watchlist plus 158 more below the floor, 198 rows in all, each with a stated implied value and a concrete six-step farm plan with gas costs and times. The value follows from the snapshot's own figures — TVL, 7-day growth, age, chains — fit at run time; no protocol is ranked by its name. Wrapped products of tokens that already trade are left out on purpose, because ranking them as launches would be wrong. The dashboard is static, sortable and filterable, and every detail view shows the value build-up in numbers.
